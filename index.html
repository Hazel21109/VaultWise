Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about budgeting? We've got answers.

What is the 50/30/20 budget rule?

It’s a simple budgeting framework where 50% of your net income goes to Needs (rent, groceries, bills), 30% to Wants (dining out, entertainment), and 20% directly to Savings and debt repayment.

How much should my emergency fund be?

Financial experts generally recommend saving 3 to 6 months' worth of essential living expenses in a high-yield savings account for ultimate peace of mind.

Where should I keep my savings?

Keep short-term savings and emergency funds in an accessible High-Yield Savings Account (HYSA) so your money earns competitive interest while remaining fully liquid.
